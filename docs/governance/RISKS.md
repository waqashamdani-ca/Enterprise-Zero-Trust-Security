## Handling and escalation

Resolve blocking risk before dependent work. Record new triggers, owner, severity, mitigation/evidence and residual acceptance. Unexpected protected connectivity, uncertain destructive targets, exposed secrets or missing recovery stop the affected work. Use approved isolation, preserve private evidence and obtain exact owner scope before further change. No implementation, procurement, license selection or risk acceptance is implied.
