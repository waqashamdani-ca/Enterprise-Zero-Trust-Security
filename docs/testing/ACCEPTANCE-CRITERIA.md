# Focused acceptance criteria and approval gates

Use these criteria to decide whether the lab meets the assignment. The analyst must be able to read reports, the engineer must be able to read maintenance instructions, and the unassigned user must be denied. The lab must also demonstrate isolation, restricted administration, remote access and application recovery. [Assignment](../assignments/PROJECT-ASSIGNMENT.md) · [Glossary](../guides/GLOSSARY.md)

Obtain explicit owner approval before each implementation stage A-L. Record the stage, scope, assets, permitted changes, validation and rollback. Service migration, shutdown and destructive recovery need approval for the exact assets and recovery procedure. Destructive disk work also needs the exact disk identity and a recoverable backup plan. Verify lawful software and provider terms. Keep service-dependent checks NOT TESTED until the approved service and evidence are available. ## Phase gates and stage approvals

Stage L's automation and recovery portions need their own approval and may precede final stage K validation.

| Phase | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Prepared Infrastructure | Prepared assets, disk and data protection, compatible versions, measured capacity, three-zone path and Dell/HP console recovery verified. Resolve isolation and readiness blockers before dependent work. |
| 2. Identity and Endpoint Security | Directory and DNS health, standard logon, effective Windows/Linux protections and separated privileges verified. |
| 3. Zero Trust Access Controls | Six application cases and revocation, one CA and server certificate, and APP1-only remote permit, deny and revoke evidence. |
| 4. Monitoring, Automation and Recovery | Five attributable events, one explained denial, one repeatable WIN1 capacity summary and one measured isolated APP1 restore. |
| 5. Validation and Final Submission | All mandatory checks pass with traceable evidence. Five completed files, rubric evaluation, reviewed publication and separate owner decision are recorded. |

### Restore approval gate

Operational restore remains **BLOCKED** until the owner approves both the encrypted external-storage backend and recovery procedure and the exact isolated physical path, disconnection and rollback checklist. Keep the restore and its unperformed checks **NOT TESTED** until approved execution supplies the required evidence. ## Engineering verification record

Record stage and approval date, scope and versions, requirement/control/test/FAC IDs, expected and actual results, verifier and date, evidence reference, second observation, defects, recovery state and verdict. Use all 91 mandatory child rows in **Deliverable 03 > Test Cases** and link their evidence in **Evidence Index**. Preserve failure evidence and record the approved correction, rollback and retest.

For network denials, require a working allowed control on the known-listening lab service. Match network and application denials to the effective rule, grant or role and its log, counter or device evidence. Use the matched administrator control for local privilege denial and the trusted and untrusted handshakes for certificate trust. A failed connection alone leaves enforcement unverified. Record the second technical observation separately from any human review.

- **PASS.** The actual result matches the expectation and all required evidence and second observations are verified.
- **FAIL.** An executed mandatory check has an observed mismatch.
- **NOT TESTED.** The check is blocked, unperformed or lacks required verification.

Overall FWT PASS requires every mandatory child to pass. Any failed child makes the overall test FAIL. Otherwise, an incomplete test remains NOT TESTED. NOT APPLICABLE cannot waive a mandatory check.

Critical failures involving isolation, privilege or authorization, Transport Layer Security (TLS) trust, remote exposure, recovery integrity, secret disclosure or false results block final acceptance at any score. Apply the 100-point rubric to verified results and retain every mandatory gate. [Evaluation rubric](../assignments/PROJECT-ASSIGNMENT.md#evaluation-rubric)

## Owner UAT record

Owner user acceptance testing (UAT) is a separate decision. Review analyst report access, engineer maintenance access, unassigned-user denial, remote access and revocation, and the recovery and evidence records. Record the reviewer, date, observed usability, limits, follow-ups and the explicit owner decision with its confirmation reference in **Deliverable 03 > Owner Acceptance**. Keep UAT **PENDING** until that decision is recorded.
